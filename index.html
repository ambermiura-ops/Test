About Amber Miura Counseling

Welcome to Amber Miura Counseling. I am so glad you are here. It is such a brave step to reach out for help. I look forward to hearing your story. I believe counseling is a sacred space shared between the therapist and client. When you sit with me it is a comfortable, non-judgmental, and empathetic environment for clients to be their most authentic selves so they can begin to grow, change, and heal. I believe in the holistic well-being of each client, addressing the mental, physical, and spiritual aspects of their lives.

I specialize in working with individual adults. In particular, I have a passion for working with women through their journey in navigating anxiety, depression, ADHD, other mood disorders, motherhood, step-motherhood, blended families, divorce, maternal mental health, pregnancy, postpartum, and infertility. I also specialize in working with elder adults as they navigate life.

Kindness, care, and compassion are at the forefront of the work I do with my clients. I believe that people are the best experts on their own lives and experiences, and it is an honor to come alongside them in their journey. I cannot wait to meet you and hear your story.

Your Mental Health and Well-Being is Everything

Free consultation: I offer a free 15-minute consultation to introduce myself, determine if we are the best fit for one another, understand why you are seeking help, and answer any initial questions you have. If I am not the right fit for you, I will send you referrals for other therapists in the area.

Sessions: Each session is tailored to provide in-depth support, lasting between 50 and 55 minutes, provided online via a secured video platform.

Fees: The self-pay rate for individual sessions is $150, ensuring affordability while maintaining the highest quality of care.
